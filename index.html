FAQ

Questions, Answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Call us at (817) 648-0690.

Do you offer free roof inspections?

Yes. Innovation Roofing offers free inspections. Call (817) 648-0690 or send an estimate request and our team will get in touch to schedule a visit.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Fort Worth, Dallas, Alvarado, and surrounding Texas communities. If you're not sure whether we cover your location, just ask when you contact us.

What roofing services do you provide?

Roof repair, roof replacement, new roof installation, roof inspection, reroofing, roof cleaning, skylight installation and repair, and attic inspection. Our team also handles gutters and general contracting.

Do you work on commercial properties?

Yes. Alongside homes, we work on commercial and organizational properties. Tell us about your building and we'll discuss the right approach.

How do I get an estimate?

Call us, or fill in the estimate request form below with your address and a short description of the project. We'll review your roofing needs, discuss options, and explain the recommended work and associated costs.

What are your business hours?

Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and Saturday, 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM. We're closed on Sunday.
